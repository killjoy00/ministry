# Privacy Policy — The Ministry of Coincidences

**Effective date:** November 19, 2026

The Ministry of Coincidences is an offline, single-player game developed by the publisher of this app.

## What the game stores
The game saves your case progress, earned Merit, department upgrades, selected settings, and accessibility preferences **on your device** so you can resume playing. This information is not associated with a Ministry account.

## Data collection and sharing
As currently built, the game does **not** collect or transmit personal information, analytics, advertising identifiers, or location data to the publisher. It contains no advertising SDK, account system, third-party analytics, or in-app purchases, and it does not require an internet connection to play.

The app does not sell or share player data with advertisers.

## Device and storefront services
Apple, Google, and the device operating system may independently collect download, purchase, diagnostic, or crash information under their own policies. Those platform operations are separate from in-game collection by The Ministry of Coincidences.

## Data retention and deletion
Your game progress is kept in local application storage on your device. Uninstalling the game generally removes this data, subject to operating-system backup behavior. A future reinstall may not recover progress unless your device backup restores it.

## Children
The game does not knowingly solicit personal information from children and has no account creation or chat feature.

## Changes
If future versions introduce network services, cloud saves, ads, analytics, or other data processing, this policy and the store privacy declarations must be updated **before** that functionality ships.

## Contact
For privacy questions about the game, please open a support issue at https://github.com/killjoy00/ministry/issues.

This policy describes the mobile game as currently implemented; review the final signed builds and store declarations before publication.
