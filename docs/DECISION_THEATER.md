# Oddford's decision theater (v0.5)

A small, animated-storybook-style scene is now the visual center of the case desk. Selecting an available incident changes three original assets at once: Oddford's panoramic setting, the character paper cutout representing that location, and the incident postcard. The document beside them shows the actual title, location, authored narration and base probability **if attempted**. It is labeled **UNDER OBSERVATION** to distinguish potential outcomes from events that have actually happened. Recorded events remain in the official chronicle and replayed town scene.

The incident tray is still scrollable, supporting the enlarged incident catalog without putting 70 cards on screen at once. Double Booking displays a 1/2 incident influence allowance, with its live filed count. Selected incident controls, the original four visual seals, effects and actual cost remain visible. The interface distinguishes *selection*, *filing*, and *advancing the day*; buttons still respond to D-pad/A, X, Y, B, LB/RB and keyboard equivalents.

## Design decisions

- **Actual world first.** `assets/illustrated/oddford_square.webp`, six existing character portraits, six existing incident families and four existing seals are used directly. No externally loaded assets or generic art substitutions.
- **The scene is a preview.** A character portrait represents the subject or incident location, not proof that a specific NPC caused an incident. No facts are generated by the view layer.
- **Clean right-hand desk.** Two rows of tactile stamp buttons leave enough room to keep the odds preview, filing status and cost in sight without a nested scrollbar. The resulting decision is still a normal intervention under `CaseSimulation`.
- **Full case coverage.** The panel works with all four assignments and with Double Booking.
- **Deck first.** The selected art stage, stamp filing controls and day-advance action fit inside 1280x800. Playtest on physical hardware remains necessary.
